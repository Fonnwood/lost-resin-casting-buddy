# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Keep discussion on topic and constructive; newcomers and hobbyists are as welcome as professionals.
- Don't publish other people's private information.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions and block participants who don't follow these rules. Report problems through GitHub's private reporting on this repository or by contacting a maintainer directly.
