Privacy policy

Casting Buddy at castingbuddyapp.com · Last updated 3 October 2026

The short version

  • Without an account, nothing you enter leaves your device. Your runs, profiles and settings are kept in your browser.
  • An account is optional. If you create one, we store your email address and the casting data you sync, and nothing else: no name, no password, no payment details.
  • Notifications are optional and need no account. If you turn them on, we store your device's push address and the times and wording of your upcoming alerts, so we can send them.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking cookies, and we never sell or share your data for marketing.
  • You can download your data or delete your account at any time from Settings.

Who we are

Casting Buddy is made and run by Fonnwood, based in the United Kingdom. Fonnwood is the data controller for the personal data described here, under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. If you are in the European Union, the EU GDPR gives you the same rights described below.

Questions or requests about your data: privacy@castingbuddyapp.com.

Casting Buddy is open source. You can read exactly what the app and server do on GitHub.

Cookies and storage on your device

  • One cookie, only if you sign in: cb_session keeps you signed in on that device for up to 30 days. It is strictly necessary for the account to work, so it doesn't need consent. Signing out removes it.
  • Browser storage: the app keeps your runs, profiles and settings in your browser's local storage so it works offline. It stays on your device and you can clear it at any time (Settings, or your browser's settings).
  • No advertising or analytics cookies, and no third-party trackers.

How long we keep it

  • Account and casting data: until you delete your account (Settings → Account & sync → Delete account). It is then removed from our database straight away, and from our database provider's automatic backups when they expire, normally within days.
  • Sign-in codes: valid for 10 minutes, deleted within about a day.
  • Sign-ins (sessions): expire after 30 days, or when you sign out.
  • Notification data: deleted when you turn notifications off. Otherwise it is deleted after 30 days without use, and sent alerts after 2 days.
  • Hosting and email logs: kept by Vercel and Resend for a limited period under their own retention policies.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your data and get a copy (Settings → Data & backup → Export full backup gives you all of your casting data at any time);
  • correct it, either in the app or by asking us;
  • delete it: Settings → Account & sync → Delete account removes your email address and everything synced, or ask us;
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • data portability: the export is a standard JSON file.

To exercise any of these, use the app or email privacy@castingbuddyapp.com. We'll reply within one month. It's free.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please tell us first. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, 0303 123 1113. In the EU, you can contact your local data protection authority.

Security

Everything is sent over HTTPS. Sign-in codes and session tokens are stored only as one-way hashes. The sign-in cookie can't be read by scripts. Sign-in attempts are limited, and notification messages are end-to-end encrypted to your device. No system is perfectly secure, but we'll tell you and the ICO promptly if a breach puts your data at risk, as the law requires.

Children

Casting Buddy is a tool for a hazardous workshop process and is not aimed at children. Please don't create an account if you are under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data, we'll update this page and the date at the top, and for significant changes we'll say so in the app.
